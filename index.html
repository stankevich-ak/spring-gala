Common Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Anything else? Reach out on Instagram.

What should I wear?+
Costumes are required — attire is part of what creates the immersive atmosphere of the Ball. We encourage romantic, spring-inspired, historical, or fantasy styles: flowing gowns, fitted jackets, floral details, period silhouettes. Detailed inspiration is available on our Instagram at @nocturnegala.
Do I need to know how to dance?+
Not at all. No prior experience is required. Everything is taught during rehearsals, step by step. The event is designed so that complete beginners feel confident and fully part of the evening.
Do I need to bring a partner?+
No partner needed. You can attend on your own or with someone. Partner rotation ensures everyone participates and connects with multiple people. Many attendees come solo — it's one of the best parts of the event.
When are the rehearsals, and what if I can't attend all of them?+
Rehearsals take place on April 14, 21, 28 and May 5 — every Tuesday at 7:30 PM at Taylor Park Elementary, Burnaby. Attending at least three rehearsals is recommended, but not required. Video tutorials are provided so you can review the material at your own pace.
What are Leaders and Followers?+
  • Leaders (traditionally the "gentleman's part") guide direction, timing, and figures through clear, caring connection.
  • Followers (traditionally the "lady's part") receive and shape the movement, responding to the lead while bringing their own musicality and expression.

Roles are open to all genders. Choose the one that feels most natural — neither is more advanced.

Which dances will be at the Ball?+
  • Group dances: Hole in the Wall, Polka for Three, Upon a Summer's Day
  • Couple dances: Waltz, Figure Waltz
  • Ceremonial: Polonaise
  • Rotational partner dances: Menuet, Spanish Waltz, Cotillon
Is this a dating event?+
The Spring Ball is primarily a social and cultural experience. That said, it naturally creates opportunities to meet like-minded people in a structured, comfortable setting. If you're attending solo and open to meeting others, you'll receive a flower pin to make introductions easier and more natural.
What is the age restriction?+
This is an 18+ event.
I'm interested as a Follower — can I still join?+
Follower spots are currently waitlisted. Join the waitlist here and you'll be notified if spots open. Leader and Couples tickets are still available and filling quickly.
